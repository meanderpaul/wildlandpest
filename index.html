Flagship platform

BAIO — Business All-in-One

BAIO combines our CRM, Workforce Hub, Inventory Manager and sales tools into one installable program. Records you enter once flow everywhere they're needed. A sold job feeds commissions, commissions feed payroll, and a completed visit takes the bait it used out of stock.

Customers & service

Customer records, recurring service due dates, scheduled routes and a call log. Track station counts and bait types per account.

Sales pipeline

Move leads from inquiry to appointment to won on a board. Won leads become sold jobs, and a leaderboard keeps the team honest.

Billing

See unpaid customers, log payment errors and read AR aging in one place instead of across spreadsheets.

Commissions to payroll

Split commissions between the seller and the tech who did the work, add manager overrides, withhold or deduct, then send the lines straight to payroll.

Team & time

One employee list with time clock, time off, PTO accrual and payroll, so office and field staff are all in the same system.

Inventory & trucks

Shop and vehicle stock, low-stock alerts, purchase orders and movement history. Services carry a bill of materials that deducts on each visit.

Industry packs. BAIO ships with a pest control pack (bait types, stations, target species, service types) and can switch to plumbing or general service labels, so it fits the way your shop already talks.

In the field

BAIO Mobile

Your techs carry BAIO on their phones. The desktop app pushes customer and station data to BAIO Mobile so bait station work can be tracked on site.

  • Bait station tracking synced from the customers in BAIO.
  • Phone Access for inventory: scan a QR code on your office network and techs can take or return shop stock, check their truck and log what they used on a job.
  • Role-based: technicians and office staff each see the actions that fit their job.
In production. Coming to Google Play.
